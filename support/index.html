Questions people ask

Is my contribution a charitable donation?

No. The site is not a registered charity, and contributions are not tax-deductible. They are voluntary support for an independent website.

Do supporters get anything in return?

Our thanks, and a site that stays free. Support never buys coverage, results or contest outcomes.

Can a business support the site?

Yes, through sponsorship of a contest prize or a section. Use the business contact page.
