Receive bulk gift requests

If you engrave, embroider, print or assemble personalized gifts in quantity, tell us what you do. We pass on briefs that match.

How it works

Buyers planning ten or more gifts send us one brief: occasion, quantity, budget, date and what should go on each piece. We match it to suppliers by what they make, where they ship and their minimum order.

You receive the brief and contact the buyer directly. The sale, the proofs and the customer are yours.

What we look for

  • You personalize in-house or control the process
  • You can state a minimum order and a normal production time
  • You send proofs before production
  • You reply to enquiries within two working days

Cost

Applying is free. Referral terms, where they apply, are agreed with you before any request is sent. We never charge buyers.
