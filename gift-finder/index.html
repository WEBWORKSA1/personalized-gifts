        Questions people ask

        How does the gift finder choose?

        It scores 120 ideas against the five things you tell it: who the gift is for, the occasion, your budget, their interests and how soon you need it.

        Does it recommend shops or products?

        No. It suggests kinds of gift and what to put on each one. You choose where to buy or whether to make it.

        Are my answers stored?

        Only in the page address, so that you can share or bookmark your results, and saved ideas stay in your browser.
