Privacy policy

Short version: tools run on your device, forms go to our inbox only when you press send, and adverts are served by Google.

This policy applies to Personalized.Gifts ("we", "the site"). It was last updated on November 19, 2026.

Information that stays on your device

The tools save some things in your browser's local storage so that they work between pages and visits: the name you type on the home page, your saved ideas, your gift list, your own dates, your quote draft and whether you have dismissed the cookie notice. This information is not sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing site data in your browser.

The photo size calculator reads the width and height of a photo you choose. The photo itself is never uploaded.

The gift exchange generator runs entirely in your browser. Names are placed in the private links you send; they are not stored by us.

Information you send us

When you submit a form (contact, quote request, supplier application, contest entry, support pledge, contributor application or newsletter sign-up) the answers you typed are delivered to our inbox by a third-party form relay service, which processes the message on our behalf. We use what you send only for the purpose of that form:

  • Quote requests: to find suitable suppliers. With the consent you give on the form, we share the request with those suppliers so that they can contact you with quotes.
  • Contest entries: to judge the round and, with your consent, to publish the winning entry with a first name and town.
  • Newsletter: to send the occasional email you asked for. Every email explains how to stop.
  • Everything else: to reply to you.

We do not sell personal information. We keep messages for as long as needed to deal with them and to keep ordinary business records, then delete them.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show adverts. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on those visits.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some other vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Google explains how it uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent by Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown.

Videos

Videos are embedded from YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. Once you play a video, YouTube may set cookies and collect information under Google's privacy policy.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details when pages are loaded, under its own privacy statement.

Children

The site is for adults choosing and making gifts. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Forms ask that entrants and applicants be 18 or over.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw a consent you gave, by writing to us through the contact form. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
