Questions people ask

What does it cost?

Nothing. Sending a brief is free and you are under no obligation to buy. Suppliers may pay us a referral fee.

Who sees my request?

We read it first. If it suits suppliers we know, we share it with them, with the consent you give on the form, and they contact you directly.

How quickly will I hear back?

We aim to reply within two working days. Quotes for larger or more complex orders take longer.

Is there a minimum order?

For supplier quotes, ten pieces. Published supplier minimums commonly sit at 10, 25 or 50 depending on how the gifts are branded. For fewer than ten, the gift finder is the better route.

Can every gift carry a different name?

Yes. Say so on the form. Individual names add cost and checking time, so allow an extra week.

How far ahead should I ask?

Six to eight weeks before you need delivery. Production alone is commonly quoted at two to six weeks.
