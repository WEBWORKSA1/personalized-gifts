Questions people ask

What counts as a personalized gift?

Any gift that carries something particular to the person: a name, initials, a date, a place, a photograph or their own handwriting. It can be engraved, stitched, printed or made by hand.

Does Personalized.Gifts sell gifts?

No. It is a free set of tools and guides. You decide what to give and what it should say here, then buy from any maker or shop you choose, or make it yourself.

How early should I order a personalized gift?

As a rule, 10 days ahead for printed gifts, 14 for engraved or embroidered gifts and 28 for custom art. The occasion calendar works out the dates for you.

What should I engrave?

A name and a date is always enough. For a line, keep it under six words and in your own voice. The engraving ideas tool suggests lines that fit the space.

In what order do monogram initials go?

With a larger centre letter: first name, surname, middle name. With letters all one size: first, middle, surname. The monogram maker arranges them for you.

Is it really free?

Yes. The site is paid for by advertising, supplier referrals, sponsorship and supporters. There is no account and no charge for any tool or download.
