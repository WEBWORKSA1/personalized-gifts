Questions people ask

Is this a real voucher for a shop?

No. It is a personal promise from you. Nothing here can be redeemed with a business.

What should I promise?

Time and effort they would not ask for: a planned day out, a chore taken over for a month, a meal cooked.

Can children make one?

Yes. Print the coupons and let them fill in or decorate each one.
