    Questions people ask

    How are the order-by dates worked out?

    They are rules of thumb: 28 days ahead for custom art, 14 for engraved and embroidered gifts, 10 for printed gifts. A maker’s own cut-off always wins.

    Why do some festival dates say expected?

    Festivals that follow lunar calendars can move by a day depending on location or moon sighting.

    Does this page update itself?

    Yes. Dates are calculated each time the page loads, and the site rebuilds itself daily.
