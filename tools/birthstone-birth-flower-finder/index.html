Questions people ask

Why do some months have more than one birthstone?

The jewellers’ list has been extended over time. June, August, October, November and December each have alternatives.

Which list of birthstones is this?

The modern list first standardised by jewellers in 1912 and revised since, with the older traditional stone shown where it differs.

Are zodiac dates exact?

They are the usual dates. A birthday on the first or last day of a sign can fall either side in some years.
