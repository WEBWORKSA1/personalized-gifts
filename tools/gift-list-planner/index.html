Questions people ask

Where is my list stored?

Only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to us, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Can I use it on my phone and laptop?

Each device keeps its own list. Use the CSV download to move it.

Does it remind me?

The calendar export adds each date to your own calendar with a reminder 14 days before.
