Questions people ask

What do the ratings mean?

Sharp is 300 pixels per inch or more, good is 200 or more, fine from a distance is 150 or more, and soft is below that.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. It is opened by your own browser only to read its dimensions.

My photo is rated soft at the size I want. What can I do?

Find the original file, choose a smaller size, or choose canvas or fabric, which hide softness better.
