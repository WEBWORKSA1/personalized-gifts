Questions people ask

What paper works best?

Card of 200 to 300 gsm. Most home printers feed it through the rear or manual tray.

Can each tag have a different name?

Make one, print, change the name and print again. To save card, use the PNG download and arrange several in a document.

Why is the print blank or cut off?

Set scale to 100% or actual size in the print dialog and turn on background graphics.
