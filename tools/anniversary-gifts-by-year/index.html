Questions people ask

What is the difference between traditional and modern themes?

The traditional list is older and names materials such as paper and wood. The modern list was added in the twentieth century with practical alternatives such as clocks and appliances.

Do the themes differ by country?

Slightly. For example the fourth year is fruit or flowers in the United States and linen or silk in the United Kingdom.

Is there a theme for every year?

For years 1 to 25, then every fifth year to 60, and the 70th.
