Questions people ask

How is 2026 written in Roman numerals?

MMXXVI: two thousands, two tens, a five and a one.

Which date order should I use?

The one the recipient reads. Day first in most countries, month first in the United States.

How accurate are coordinates with four decimals?

To about ten metres, enough to mark a doorway.
