    Questions people ask

    How are characters counted?

    The way engravers count: every letter, number, space and punctuation mark on a line.

    Are these inscriptions free to use?

    Yes. Every line was written for this site and is yours to engrave, stitch or print on anything.

    Why does a line say it does not fit?

    It is longer than the characters or lines you set. Shorten it, choose a larger object, or raise the limit if your maker allows more.
