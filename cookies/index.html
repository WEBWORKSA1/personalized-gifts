Cookie policy

We set no cookies of our own. Advertising and video partners may, and you control both.

Last updated on November 19, 2026.

What we store ourselves

Personalized.Gifts does not set cookies. It uses your browser's local storage, which stays on your device, to remember:

ItemPurpose
NameShows the name you typed on gift previews across the site
Saved ideasKeeps your shortlist of gift ideas
Gift list and your datesStores the planner and calendar entries you add
Quote draftKeeps your answers if you leave the quote form part-way
NoticeRemembers that you closed the cookie notice

Cookies set by others

Google AdSense and its advertising partners use cookies to show and measure adverts, including adverts based on your visits to this and other sites. Manage this in Google's Ads Settings or at aboutads.info.

YouTube may set cookies when you press play on an embedded video. Videos do not load until you do.

Controlling cookies

You can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. The tools continue to work without cookies. If you clear local storage, saved lists and drafts are removed.
