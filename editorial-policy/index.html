Editorial policy and how we make money

Advice first. Advertising, referrals and sponsorship pay for it and have no say in it.

Last updated on November 19, 2026.

How content is made

Guides and tools start from a practical question people ask when personalizing a gift. Facts such as birthstone lists, anniversary themes, monogram conventions and date rules are checked against published references. Practical figures such as character limits and production times are drawn from what makers themselves publish, and are described as ranges because they vary.

Some content on this site is produced with the help of AI tools. Whatever the method, the standard is the same: accurate, specific and useful. Suggested inscriptions and poem lines are original to this site.

How the site earns money

  • Advertising. Adverts are served by Google AdSense and labelled as advertisements. We do not choose individual adverts.
  • Referral fees. When we pass a bulk quote request to a supplier, the supplier may pay us a fee. You never pay us.
  • Sponsorship. A sponsor may fund a contest prize or a section of the site. Sponsored material is labelled.
  • Supporters. Readers can contribute to running costs on the support page.

What money cannot do

Advertisers, suppliers and sponsors do not review content before it is published and cannot buy a recommendation. Tools give the same results to everyone. We never ask visitors to click on adverts.

Corrections

If something is wrong, tell us through the contact form. We fix confirmed errors promptly and update the date on the page.
