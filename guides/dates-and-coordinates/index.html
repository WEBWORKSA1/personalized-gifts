Roman numerals, coordinates and other quiet ways to mark a day

How to write a date in Roman numerals, format coordinates for engraving, and avoid the common mistakes.

By the Personalized.Gifts editors. Updated November 19, 2026. How we research

In short

  • Roman numerals turn a date into pattern: 14 February 2026 becomes XIV.II.MMXXVI.
  • Coordinates need both a latitude and a longitude, each with its N/S or E/W letter.
  • Decide the day-month order before you convert; numerals hide the difference.

Not every gift should announce what it celebrates. A date in Roman numerals or a pair of coordinates looks like decoration to a stranger and means everything to the wearer.

Roman numerals in one minute

Seven letters do all the work: I is 1, V is 5, X is 10, L is 50, C is 100, D is 500 and M is 1000. Write the largest values first and add them up. A smaller letter placed before a larger one is subtracted, which is how 4 becomes IV and 9 becomes IX.

Writing a full date

Convert the day, the month and the year separately, then join them with dots, small centred dots or dashes. The fourteenth of February 2026 is XIV.II.MMXXVI. Years take the most room: 2026 is seven letters, 1988 is eleven. If space is tight, use the last two digits of the year or drop the year.

Decide the order first. Day-month-year is usual in most of the world; month-day-year in the United States. With numerals, II.III could be the second of March or the third of February, and no one can tell afterwards.

Coordinates

A location is two numbers: latitude, north or south of the equator, and longitude, east or west of Greenwich. They are written in one of two ways.

  • Decimal degrees: 45.5019° N, 73.5674° W. Compact and easy to check.
  • Degrees, minutes and seconds: 45°30′07″N 73°34′03″W. The classic engraved look.

To find them, press and hold on the spot in a maps app; the coordinates appear in the search bar or the place details. Four decimal places are accurate to about ten metres, enough to mark a doorway.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Dropping the minus sign or the letter. 45.5, 73.5 without W lands in Kazakhstan, not Canada.
  • Swapping latitude and longitude. Latitude comes first and never exceeds 90.
  • Rounding too far. Two decimal places is a neighbourhood; four is a front door.

Other quiet marks

A birth flower in place of a name. The time on a watch-face print set to the moment a child was born. A postcode. Initials in the order only family would recognise. Each says the thing without saying it aloud.

Questions people ask

How do I write the year 2000 in Roman numerals?

MM. The year 2026 is MMXXVI and 1999 is MCMXCIX.

Is there a zero in Roman numerals?

No. If you shorten a year ending in 00, write the full year.

Which coordinate format should I engrave?

Degrees, minutes and seconds look traditional; decimals are shorter. Both point to the same place.
