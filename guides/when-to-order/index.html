How early to order a personalized gift

Realistic lead times for engraved, embroidered, printed and custom-art gifts, and how to count back from the day.

By the Personalized.Gifts editors. Updated November 19, 2026. How we research

In short

  • Rule of thumb: printed gifts 10 days, engraved or embroidered 14 days, custom art 28 days before you need it.
  • Add a week in the month before Christmas, Mother’s Day and Valentine’s Day.
  • If you are inside the window, print something today and give the made piece later.

A personalized gift is made after you order it. That single fact explains every late parcel. The item does not exist yet, the maker has a queue, and nothing can be swapped from a shelf if it goes wrong.

What the time is made of

Three stages add up. Proofing is the back-and-forth to approve the design, from zero to three days and faster if you reply at once. Production runs from one day for printed items to several weeks for hand-drawn art. Delivery is the part you already understand, and the only part express shipping shortens.

These are planning figures, not promises. Individual makers on marketplaces often quote one to three working days for simple engraved pieces; studios producing branded gift boxes quote two to six weeks. Always read the estimate on the listing itself.

Counting back

  1. Start from the day you will hand it over, not the occasion. If you travel on the 22nd, that is your date.
  2. Subtract two days for wrapping and the unexpected.
  3. Subtract the lead time from the table.
  4. If the date you land on has passed, change the plan, not the calendar.

The busy months

From mid-November every maker is working at capacity, and published Christmas cut-offs fall anywhere from late November to mid-December. The weeks before Mother's Day and Valentine's Day are the next busiest. Add a week in those periods, and order custom art before the rush begins.

Sending abroad

International parcels add customs. Allow four to six weeks in total, and check whether the recipient will be charged duty; a gift that arrives with a bill is not the effect you want. A maker in the recipient's own country removes the problem.

When you are already late

Give a promise, properly presented. Print a certificate describing the gift that is on its way, put it in an envelope with their name on it, and hand over the real thing when it arrives. People enjoy the second moment as much as the first.

Questions people ask

What is the latest I can order for Christmas?

It depends on the maker. As a plan, order custom art by mid-November, engraved and stitched gifts by the first week of December and printed gifts by about the 12th.

Does paying for express delivery help?

It shortens delivery only. If production takes a week, express shipping does not change that.

How long do bulk personalized orders take?

Allow six to eight weeks from first enquiry: time to agree the design, two to six weeks of production, then delivery.
