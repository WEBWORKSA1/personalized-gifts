Will my photo print well? Pixels by print size

How many pixels a photo needs for mugs, prints, canvases and blankets, and how to check a file before you order.

By the Personalized.Gifts editors. Updated November 19, 2026. How we research

In short

  • Aim for 300 pixels per inch on anything held in the hand, and 150 on anything hung on a wall.
  • Divide the photo’s pixel width by the print width in inches to get its pixels per inch.
  • Photos saved from social media or messaging apps are usually too small. Get the original.

A photo is a grid of pixels. Print it small and the pixels are packed tightly and the picture looks sharp. Stretch the same grid across a blanket and each pixel becomes a visible square. The only question is how far your file can stretch.

The one sum

Pixels per inch equals the photo's width in pixels divided by the print's width in inches. A 4,000-pixel-wide photo printed 10 inches wide gives 400 pixels per inch, which is excellent. Printed 40 inches wide, it gives 100, which is soft.

How many is enough

Pixels per inchResultUse it for
300 or moreSharp at reading distancePhoto books, cards, mugs, small prints
200 to 299GoodFramed prints, calendars
150 to 199Fine from a step backCanvas, posters, blankets
Under 150Visibly softChoose a smaller size

Viewing distance is why the bar drops for large pieces. Nobody reads a canvas from 30 centimetres away, and woven or fleece fabric cannot hold fine detail anyway.

Finding your photo's size

On a phone, open the photo and look at its details or info panel; the dimensions are listed in pixels. On a computer, right-click the file and choose properties or get info. Or drop the file into our photo size calculator, which reads the dimensions on your own device without uploading anything.

Why a good-looking photo can still fail

  • It was sent through a messaging app. Most shrink photos to save data. Ask for the original by email or a shared album.
  • It was saved from a social feed. Those copies are small and heavily compressed.
  • It was cropped. Cropping throws pixels away. A face cut from a group shot may be only a few hundred pixels wide.
  • It is a screenshot. A screenshot has the pixels of your screen, not of the photo.

Other things printers reject

Dark, noisy pictures taken indoors at night look worse in print than on a bright screen. Leave a margin around faces, because products trim or wrap the edges. And check the shape: a square photo on a tall phone case loses its sides.

Questions people ask

How many megapixels do I need?

Twelve megapixels, the size most phones have produced for years, is enough for a sharp print up to about 9 x 13 inches and a good canvas up to about 20 x 27.

Can I enlarge a small photo with software?

Upscaling tools can help a little, but they invent detail. For faces, a smaller sharp print is better than a large guessed one.

Is the calculator private?

Yes. The photo is read by your browser to get its width and height and is never sent anywhere.
