Personalized graduation gifts

Personalized graduation gift ideas that mark the name, the award and the year.

Graduation gifts mark a new name: the first time a person signs with letters after it, or starts a job with a title. Pens, card cases, notebooks and frames are traditional because they go straight to work.

Copy the award from the certificate exactly, including the abbreviation the institution uses. For school leavers, a compass, key ring or luggage tag points at what happens next.

What to put on it

  • Full name and award, exactly as on the certificate
  • Class year
  • A line about where they are going, not where they have been

Questions people ask

What is a good graduation engraving?

Name, award and year on something formal. On something personal, a short line from you with the date.

How formal should the name be?

Formal on frames and pens, since those are for work. First name on anything they will carry.
