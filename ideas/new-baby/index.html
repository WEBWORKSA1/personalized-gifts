Personalized gifts for a new baby

Personalized new baby gift ideas, with the birth details to collect and the date to order.

The arrival of a baby is the one occasion where waiting is correct. Order after the name is announced, and take the details from the announcement itself. A gift two weeks after the birth with the right name beats one on the day with the wrong one.

Give something that records the facts, and something for the nursery. Parents of second and third children especially notice when the new baby gets a keepsake of their own.

What to put on it

  • Full name, date, time, weight and length
  • Birthstone or birth flower for the month
  • A stocking or ornament for the first Christmas, ordered early

Questions people ask

What if the baby’s name is not announced yet?

Print a certificate promising the gift, or give something monogram-free now and the named piece later.

What do you give for a second baby?

The same keepsake the first child has, with the new name. Siblings compare.
