Personalized gifts for kids

Personalized gift ideas for children, with advice on names, spelling and what is safe to print.

Children are delighted by their own name. For the first few years, a name on a stool, a book or a stocking is the whole present. From school age, the name becomes useful too: on bottles, bags and labels that come home again.

Two cautions. Check the spelling with a parent, including capital letters inside the name. And keep full names off anything a child wears in public; initials or a first name inside a flap do the job without telling strangers who they are.

What to put on it

  • The name spelled the way the child writes it
  • Lowercase letters for children who are learning to read
  • Initials, not full names, on outdoor clothing and bags

Questions people ask

What age is right for a name gift?

Any age. Babies get keepsakes the parents enjoy, toddlers get things they can point at, and school-age children get things that stop being lost.

Is it safe to put a child’s name on a backpack?

Safety guidance commonly advises against a visible full name on bags and coats. Use initials outside and put the name and a phone number on a label inside.
