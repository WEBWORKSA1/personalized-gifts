Personalized Mother’s Day gifts

Personalized Mother’s Day gift ideas, with the date for your country and when to order.

Mother’s Day falls on different dates around the world: the fourth Sunday of Lent in the UK and Ireland, the second Sunday of May in the US, Canada, Australia, India and many other countries. Check which applies before you count the days.

The gifts that land are about her children. Their names, their birthstones, their handwriting, their drawings. If the children are small, help them make it; if they are grown, organise them.

What to put on it

  • Children’s names or initials, in birth order
  • A child’s drawing or handwriting, copied as it is
  • Birthstones for each child on one piece
