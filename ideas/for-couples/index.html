Personalized gifts for couples

Personalized gift ideas for couples, with the etiquette of joint monograms and shared surnames.

A gift for two needs a detail that belongs to both. Dates and places work for every couple. Names and monograms need a quick check first: whether they share a surname, and which name goes first, are decisions that belong to them.

For the home, think of what gets used when people come round: boards, glasses, a doormat. For the wall, a map or night sky of the day they met is hard to beat.

What to put on it

  • Both first names joined by an ampersand
  • The shared surname and the year they married, if they share one
  • A place and date instead of names when you are unsure

Questions people ask

Whose initial goes first in a couple’s monogram?

There is no single rule today. The traditional layout puts one first initial each side of a larger shared surname initial. Try both orders in the monogram maker and use the one that looks balanced.

What if they have different surnames?

Use two first initials with an ampersand, or the two surnames with a small dot between. Avoid inventing a shared surname for them.
