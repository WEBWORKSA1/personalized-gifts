Personalized Valentine’s Day gifts

Personalized Valentine’s Day gift ideas, from engraved jewellery to coupons you can print tonight.

Valentine’s Day, 14 February, rewards the small and specific. A date, a place, a sentence. It does not need to be expensive; it needs to be about the two of you and nobody else.

January is a slow month for makers, so engraved pieces ordered by the end of the month usually arrive in time. After that, print something: a coupon book or a certificate for an evening you plan yourself.

What to put on it

  • The date you met, in Roman numerals
  • Coordinates of the first date
  • Six printed coupons, one of them ridiculous

Questions people ask

What is a thoughtful Valentine’s gift that is not jewellery?

A map or night-sky print of where you met, a jar of written reasons, or a printed certificate for a planned day.

Is it too soon for a personalized gift?

In a new relationship, keep it light: initials or an in-joke, not names and dates in metal.
