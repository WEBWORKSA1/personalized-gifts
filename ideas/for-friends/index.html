Personalized gifts for friends

Personalized gift ideas for friends that are thoughtful without being heavy.

Friendship gifts work best when they are light: an in-joke on a mug, a plant pot with a silly name, initials on something useful. You are saying “I noticed”, not “I spent”.

A group of friends is the best case for matching sets with different names. Same cup, same tote, same key ring, one each. It costs little and turns a hen weekend, a team or a book club into a set.

What to put on it

  • The joke only your group gets, in three words
  • Matching items with each person’s own name
  • The date of the trip, the gig or the day you met

Questions people ask

How much should I spend on a personalized gift for a friend?

Under $25 covers most of the good ones. The personal detail does the work that money does in other gifts.

Is a funny engraving a bad idea?

Not if it will still be funny in five years. Avoid anything about a current partner, job or haircut.
