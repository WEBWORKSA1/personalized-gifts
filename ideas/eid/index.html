Personalized Eid gifts

Personalized Eid gift ideas, from named envelopes for children to keepsakes for the home.

Eid gifts centre on children and on visiting. Named envelopes for gift money, sweet boxes with a printed sleeve, and small pieces of jewellery with a name are all traditional starting points.

The date depends on the sighting of the moon, so plan for the earliest likely day. Printable envelopes and tags are the safe choice for anything decided in the last week.

What to put on it

  • Each child’s name on their own envelope
  • A family greeting and the year on a sweet box sleeve
  • A name in the script the family reads

Questions people ask

When is Eid al-Fitr next?

The occasion calendar shows the expected date. It can move by a day depending on the moon sighting where you live.

Can a name be written in Arabic or Urdu script?

Many makers offer it. Ask a fluent reader to check the proof before it is made.
