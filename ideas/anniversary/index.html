Personalized anniversary gifts

Personalized anniversary gift ideas by year, with traditional and modern themes and what to engrave.

Every anniversary year has a traditional material and a modern alternative, from paper for the first to diamond for the sixtieth. They are a prompt, not a rule, and they make choosing easier: you only have to decide what to say on it.

The details that work every year are the date, the place, and the count. A date in Roman numerals, the coordinates of the venue, or “year nine” on the back of something small.

What to put on it

  • The wedding date, in digits or Roman numerals
  • Coordinates of the venue or the place you met
  • A line from your vows, in your own words
