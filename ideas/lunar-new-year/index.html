Personalized Lunar New Year gifts

Personalized Lunar New Year gift ideas: named envelopes, seals and family keepsakes.

Lunar New Year gifts are given between generations. Envelopes with each child’s name, a carved name seal for an elder, and anything that carries the family name are traditional directions.

The year’s zodiac animal gives you a second detail. If names are written in characters, have a fluent reader confirm them before carving or printing; seals cannot be corrected.

What to put on it

  • Each child’s name on a red envelope
  • The family name in the characters the family uses
  • The animal of the year being welcomed
