Personalized housewarming gifts

Personalized housewarming gift ideas: address stamps, house portraits, boards and signs.

A new home comes with a new address, and that is your personalization. Stamps, signs and prints that carry the address or its coordinates are useful in the first month and sentimental by the tenth year.

Check the details with the owners: the exact address format, the house name if it has one, and the date they count as moving in. For a first home, that date matters to them.

What to put on it

  • Surname and the year they moved in
  • The address or its coordinates
  • A drawing of the house, captioned with the date

Questions people ask

What is a good housewarming gift for a couple with different surnames?

Use first names, the house name or the address. A doormat with both surnames also works and helps the post arrive.

What should I avoid?

Large wall art in your taste. Keep personalized pieces small or useful.
