Custom art and illustrated gift ideas

Custom art gift ideas: portraits, maps, night skies and prints made from your details.

Custom art turns facts into a picture: a date becomes a night sky, an address becomes a map, a family becomes a drawing. Because a person makes it, it takes longest of all the personalized categories. Three to four weeks is normal, and December is longer.

Your part is the brief. Send clear photographs, exact spellings, and one sentence about the mood you want. Ask to see a proof, and reply the same day.

What to put on it

  • Exact date, place and time for sky and map prints
  • One clear photo per person or pet
  • A short caption: names and a date

Questions people ask

How far ahead should I order custom art?

A month before you need it, and six weeks before Christmas. Hand-drawn portraits have the longest queues.

What if I do not like the proof?

Say so at once and be specific. Most artists include one round of changes; ask before you order.
