Personalized Hanukkah gifts

Personalized Hanukkah gift ideas for eight nights, with the next festival date.

Eight nights suit small gifts, and small gifts suit a name. One keepsake with the year for the first night, then printed tags and envelopes for the rest, is a plan that works for most families.

Hanukkah begins at sundown and moves through late November and December from year to year, so check the date before you count back for delivery.

What to put on it

  • A child’s name and the year on one lasting piece
  • Eight printed tags, numbered by night
  • The family name on something for the table
