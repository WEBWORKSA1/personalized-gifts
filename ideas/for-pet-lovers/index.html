Personalized gifts for pet lovers

Personalized gift ideas for people and their pets: portraits, tags and keepsakes with the pet’s name.

For a pet owner, the pet’s name is the personalization. Put it on the bowl, the tag, the stocking and the portrait, and you have covered a birthday and a Christmas. The owner’s name is optional.

Portraits depend entirely on the photograph. Ask for, or quietly take, a picture at the animal’s eye level in daylight, with the whole head in frame. Phone snapshots from above rarely work.

What to put on it

  • The pet’s name, in capitals, as called across the park
  • Two phone numbers on any tag worn outdoors
  • For a memorial: name, years, and what they did best

Questions people ask

What do I need for a pet portrait?

One sharp, well-lit photo at eye level, plus a note of collar colour and any markings the photo hides. Allow three weeks or more.

What is a kind gift when a pet has died?

A framed photograph with the collar tag, a garden marker, or a donation in the pet’s name with a printed card. Keep the wording short.
