Personalized gifts for coworkers and clients

Personalized gift ideas for colleagues, teams and clients, with advice on names, logos and budgets.

At work, personalize with the recipient’s name, not your logo. A notebook with their initials is a gift. The same notebook with your company mark is stationery. If the brand must appear, put it small on the packaging or the card.

Keep it neutral and useful: desk things, drinkware, bags. Check the spelling against their email signature, and check your company’s gift policy and theirs before sending anything to a client.

What to put on it

  • Their name or initials, spelled as in their signature
  • A date that marks the project, the deal or the years of service
  • Your logo on the box or card, not on the object

Questions people ask

Buying for a whole team?

Ten or more of the same item usually counts as a bulk order, with its own minimums and lead times. Use the quote request and we will pass your brief to suppliers who do this.

Is a personalized gift appropriate for a client?

Usually, if it is modest and useful. Many organisations cap the value of gifts their staff may accept, so ask before you send.
