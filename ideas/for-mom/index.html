Personalized gifts for mom

Personalized gift ideas for mothers, with what to engrave, stitch or print on each one.

Mothers tend to keep things. The gifts that last are the ones that record the family: every child’s name, the handwriting of someone she misses, the recipe only she can make. Start from what she already does every day, then put the family on it.

If she has more than one child, count carefully. A ring with three stones for four children is the gift she remembers for the wrong reason. Stepchildren, grandchildren and the dog are her call, so ask a sibling if you are unsure.

What to put on it

  • Children’s names in birth order, or their initials on separate charms
  • A recipe or a note in her own mother’s handwriting
  • The name the family really uses for her, not “Mother”

Questions people ask

What is a good personalized gift for a mom who says she wants nothing?

Something that records the family and needs no storage: a framed name poem, a birthstone piece, or a photo book of the year. Mothers who want nothing usually mean nothing that adds clutter.

Should I use “Mom”, “Mum” or her first name?

Use what her children call her out loud. Her first name suits jewellery she wears to work; the family name for her suits things that stay at home.
