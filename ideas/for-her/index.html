Personalized gifts for her

Personalized gift ideas for a wife, girlfriend, sister or daughter, with wording that sounds like you.

The strongest gifts for a partner, sister or daughter say something only the two of you would understand. A place, a date, a phrase from a text message. Jewellery and leather carry these best because they are worn and handled daily.

Check the details that are easy to get wrong: the spelling she uses, the metal she wears (look at what she has on today), and whether she prefers her full name or the short one.

What to put on it

  • Coordinates of the place you met, with the date on the reverse
  • Her initial, or her monogram with the surname she uses
  • A two- or three-word phrase that is yours alone

Questions people ask

Is it safe to guess her ring or necklace size?

Necklaces are forgiving; rings are not. For a ring, borrow one she wears on the same finger and measure the inside diameter, or choose an adjustable style.

What if I am not sure which initials to use?

Use her first initial alone. It is never wrong, and it works before and after any change of surname.
