Monogrammed gift ideas

Monogrammed gift ideas with the rules for initial order, couples and single letters.

A monogram is two or three initials treated as a design. The classic three-letter form puts the surname initial larger in the centre, with the first initial on the left and the middle on the right. Block styles keep all letters the same size and in reading order.

One letter is the safe choice when you are unsure of a middle name or a change of surname. Use the surname initial for household things and the first-name initial for personal ones.

What to put on it

  • Three letters: first, LAST, middle
  • Couples: one first initial each side of the shared surname
  • A single letter when in doubt

Questions people ask

What order do monogram initials go in?

With a larger centre letter: first, last, middle. With letters all one size: first, middle, last. The monogram maker arranges them for you.

Do monograms suit men and women equally?

Yes. Convention once reserved the centred style for women and the in-order style for men; today either is used by anyone.
