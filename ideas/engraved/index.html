Engraved gift ideas

Engraved gift ideas for metal, wood, glass and leather, with typical character limits.

Engraving cuts or burns the design into the surface, so it cannot peel or fade. That permanence is the appeal and the risk: there is no undo. Decide the words first, count the characters, and read the proof aloud.

Space is smaller than you expect. A ring holds 15 to 30 characters, a knife handle about 20, a mug or glass about 30 across. Short lines engrave larger and read better.

What to put on it

  • Names, initials and dates before sentences
  • Capitals for small spaces; they stay legible
  • A date in Roman numerals when you want it discreet

Questions people ask

Does engraving wear off?

Deep engraving on metal, wood and glass lasts for the life of the object. Shallow laser marking on coated items can fade with heavy use, so ask which you are buying.

Can engraved items be returned?

Usually not, unless the maker made the mistake. Check the proof carefully and keep a copy of what you approved.
