Memorial and sympathy gifts

Personalized memorial gift ideas, with gentle guidance on names, dates and wording.

A memorial gift should be quiet. A name, the dates, and at most a few words. The family will read it many times, so plain language wears best. If you are not close family, choose something small that asks nothing of them: a garden marker, a framed photograph, a candle.

Take the name and dates from the order of service or the notice, not from memory. If you are unsure whether a personalized piece is welcome, write a letter instead; it is always right.

What to put on it

  • Full name and both dates, checked against the notice
  • Their own handwriting or signature, copied faithfully
  • A plain line about what they loved

Questions people ask

How soon should a memorial gift arrive?

There is no deadline. A gift that arrives a month or two later, when the calls have stopped, is often valued more.

What wording is appropriate?

Name and dates alone are complete. If you add a line, describe the person. Our guide to memorial wording has examples.
