Personalized thank-you gifts

Personalized thank-you gift ideas for teachers, nurses, hosts, neighbours and teams.

A thank-you gift is small by nature. What makes it personal is being specific about what you are thanking them for. Put that on the tag, in your handwriting, and the object itself can be modest.

For a group, such as a ward, a class or a team, matching items with each person’s name show that you know there were individuals in it.

What to put on it

  • Their name, spelled correctly: ask someone
  • The exact thing you are thanking them for, on the tag
  • A date, if it marks the end of something

Questions people ask

How much should a thank-you gift cost?

Little. Under $25 is typical, and some professions may not accept more. A specific note carries most of the value.

Can I thank a whole team with one gift?

Yes. Something shared for the staff room with a printed card naming everyone works well.
