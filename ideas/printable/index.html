Printable personalized gifts you can make today

Personalized gifts to make and print at home in minutes: tags, certificates, coupons, poems and monograms.

When there is no time left, print it. A certificate for a planned day, a coupon book, a name poem or a framed monogram takes ten minutes and costs the price of a sheet of card. Presented properly, in a frame or an envelope, none of them look like a rescue.

Use heavier paper than you think: 200 gsm card or more. Print at actual size, trim with a ruler and blade, and sign it by hand.

What to put on it

  • Their name at the top, in the largest type
  • A specific promise with a date
  • Your signature, in ink

Questions people ask

What paper should I use?

White or cream card of 200 to 300 gsm. Most home printers take it through the rear tray.

Can I print at a shop?

Yes. Download the picture, put it on a memory stick or email it to the shop, and ask for card stock.
