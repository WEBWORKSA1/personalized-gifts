Name and initial jewellery ideas

Name, initial, birthstone and coordinates jewellery ideas, with sizing and spelling checks.

Jewellery is the most personal category because it is worn. Names, initials, birthstones and coordinates are the four standard details, and each can be mixed: an initial on the front, a date on the back.

Before ordering, check three things: the spelling they use, the metal colour they wear, and the length. Most necklaces are 40 to 45 cm; ask for an extender if you are guessing.

What to put on it

  • An initial, theirs or their child’s
  • A birthstone for each person they love
  • Coordinates on the front, the place name hidden on the back

Questions people ask

Which birthstone goes with which month?

Use the birthstone and birth flower finder. It lists the modern stone for every month and the older alternatives.

How do I measure a ring size secretly?

Borrow a ring they wear on that finger and measure the inside diameter in millimetres, or press it into a bar of soap and take that to a jeweller.
