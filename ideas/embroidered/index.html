Embroidered gift ideas

Embroidered gift ideas for towels, robes, bags and baby blankets, with thread and placement tips.

Embroidery stitches the name into the fabric, which gives it texture and a slightly formal look. It suits anything that gets washed: towels, robes, blankets, bags. Thread colour matters more than font; choose a tone from the room or the outfit, not a default.

Stitching needs width. Very small letters close up, so keep names under about twelve letters on a chest or pocket, and use initials where space is tight.

What to put on it

  • First names in script; initials in capitals
  • Thread a shade darker or lighter than the fabric for a quiet look
  • Placement off-centre, bottom right, for bags and towels

Questions people ask

Does embroidery survive washing?

Yes. Good embroidery outlasts the towel. Wash inside out and avoid ironing directly over the stitches.

How long does embroidery take?

Allow about two weeks including delivery. Before Christmas and Mother’s Day, add a week.
