Personalized gifts for dad

Personalized gift ideas for fathers: tools, leather, drinkware and the right few words for each.

Fathers are easier than their reputation. Choose something he already reaches for, such as a knife, a pen, a mug or a tape measure, and mark it as his. Useful objects with a name on them stay in service for decades, which is the point.

Keep the wording short and in the family’s own voice. Stock titles on shop shelves are written for nobody in particular. The nickname his children gave him at age four is written for him.

What to put on it

  • His children’s names or birth dates, kept small
  • The family nickname, exactly as it is said
  • A date and place: the day he became a dad, the ground where his team plays

Questions people ask

What should I engrave for my dad?

A name and a year is enough on a tool or knife. On something with more room, add four or five words in your own voice. The engraving ideas tool will suggest lines that fit the space.

He is practical. Will he actually use a personalized gift?

Yes, if it is a better version of something he owns. Engrave the handle, not the working surface, and it reads as ownership, not decoration.
