Photo gift ideas

Photo gift ideas, with the pixel sizes you need for sharp prints at each size.

A photo gift is only as good as the file. A picture that looks fine on a phone can print soft at poster size. As a rule, you want about 300 pixels for every inch of print for anything held in the hand, and 150 for canvas and wall pieces seen from across a room.

Choose one strong photograph over a collage wherever you can. Faces should be large, in daylight, and uncropped by the edges of the product.

What to put on it

  • One photo they have never seen printed
  • A caption with the place and year
  • Birthdays printed on a calendar

Questions people ask

How do I know if my photo is big enough?

Open the photo size calculator, choose the file or type its pixel size, and it shows the quality at every common print size.

Can I use a photo from social media?

Those files are compressed and often too small. Ask the person who took it to send the original.
