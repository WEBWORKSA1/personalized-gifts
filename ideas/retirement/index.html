Personalized retirement gifts

Personalized retirement gift ideas, with wording for plaques, clocks and memory books.

A retirement gift has to do two jobs: thank someone for the years, and wish them well for the next ones. The best engravings name the years and say something specific about the person, in the words the team would use.

A signed memory book outlasts every clock. Start collecting pages three weeks before the last day, and include people who have already left.

What to put on it

  • Name, role and the years of service
  • One line the team would recognise as theirs
  • Signatures: everyone’s, collected early

Questions people ask

What do you write on a retirement plaque?

Name, years, and one true sentence. “For 31 years of opening up before anyone else” says more than a stock phrase.

Who should sign the gift?

Everyone who worked with them, including former colleagues. Send a page they can sign and return.
