Personalized wedding gifts

Personalized wedding gift ideas for the couple, the wedding party and both sets of parents.

Weddings need three kinds of gift: for the couple, for the people standing up with them, and for the parents. Names and the date cover all three. For the couple, choose something for the home they will share; for the party, matching pieces with each person’s own name.

Confirm the surname question before you engrave anything. Not every couple shares one, and not every couple has decided. First names and the date are safe in every case.

What to put on it

  • Both first names and the wedding date
  • Role and date for the wedding party
  • A line of thanks for a parent, on something small they can hold on the day

Questions people ask

How early should I order a personalized wedding gift?

Four weeks before the day for engraved or stitched gifts, and six for custom art. Wedding-party sets take longer because every piece is different.

Can I use their new surname?

Only if they have told you what it will be. If in doubt, use first names.
