Personalized gifts for teachers

Personalized gift ideas for teachers that get used in the classroom and survive the summer.

Teachers receive a great many mugs. What they keep is anything with their name that stops their own supplies disappearing: stamped pencils, a tote, a notebook, a stamp for marking. A note from the child, in the child’s handwriting, matters more than the object.

Use the name the class uses, title included. “Ms Okafor” on a tote will be carried to school; a first name may not be.

What to put on it

  • Title and surname, as written on the classroom door
  • The class and year: “Class 4B, 2027”
  • A printed tag signed by the child

Questions people ask

What is an appropriate budget for a teacher gift?

Small. Under $25 is typical, and a class collection for one better gift is often welcome. Some schools set a limit, so check.

Is a handmade or printable gift enough?

Yes. A printed certificate or name poem with real, specific thanks is kept long after the chocolates are gone.
