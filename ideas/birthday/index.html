Personalized birthday gifts

Personalized birthday gift ideas for every age and budget, with what to write on them.

A birthday is the one day that belongs to a single person, so the gift should carry something that could not belong to anyone else: their name, their birth month, the number they are turning. Birthstones and birth flowers give you a detail when you barely know them.

Milestone birthdays suit a count. Thirty reasons, forty photographs, fifty signatures in a book. The number does the organising for you.

What to put on it

  • Their name and the year, on something they will keep using
  • The stone or flower of their birth month
  • A list matched to their age: one reason, photo or memory per year

Questions people ask

What is a good last-minute personalized birthday gift?

A printed certificate for a day out, a name poem in a frame, or a coupon book. All three can be made on this site and printed in ten minutes.

What do I write on a birthday engraving?

Name and date is always enough. To add a line, use five words or fewer and avoid the age; they may not want it in metal.
