Personalized gifts for him

Personalized gift ideas for a husband, boyfriend, brother or son, from leather and steel to prints.

Most men own few objects they would call theirs. A wallet, a watch, a bag, a pen. Marking one of them makes it harder to lose and easier to keep. Engraving and blind-stamped leather look the most grown-up, and both improve with wear.

Hide the sentiment. A message on the back of a watch or the inside of a wallet is read every day by one person. That is the audience you want.

What to put on it

  • Initials outside, a private line inside
  • A date in Roman numerals: no explanation needed in public
  • Coordinates of a stadium, a summit or a front door

Questions people ask

How many characters fit on a watch back or wallet?

Plan on three short lines of about 20 characters for a watch back and two or three initials for a wallet corner. The engraving ideas tool checks your wording against typical limits.

Block letters or script?

Block capitals for anything he uses at work. Script reads as jewellery, which is right for a wedding or anniversary piece.
