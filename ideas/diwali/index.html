Personalized Diwali gifts

Personalized Diwali gift ideas for family, friends and colleagues, with the next festival date.

Diwali gifts travel between households, so the family name is the natural detail: on a lamp tray, a sweet box sleeve, a brass plate. For colleagues and clients, a neutral greeting with your family or company name is usual.

The date moves each year with the lunar calendar. Order named pieces three weeks ahead; print sleeves, tags and envelopes at home in the last week.

What to put on it

  • The receiving family’s name, as they write it in English
  • Your own family name and the year on the tag
  • Each child’s name on their own envelope
