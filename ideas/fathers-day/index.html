Personalized Father’s Day gifts

Personalized Father’s Day gift ideas, with the date for your country and wording that is not a cliché.

Father’s Day is the third Sunday of June in the US, UK, Canada, India and many other countries, and the first Sunday of September in Australia and New Zealand. Engraved and leather gifts need about two weeks, so the order goes in at the start of the month.

Give him something he will use in front of other people, with a mark only he knows the meaning of. Children’s initials on a key ring. A date inside a wallet.

What to put on it

  • Children’s names or birth dates, small and out of sight
  • The name they call him at home
  • A tool, pen or knife with his name on the handle

Engraving ideas

Words for rings, pens, boards and frames, counted against the space you have.

Questions people ask

What do I engrave on a Father’s Day gift?

His name and the year, or the children’s names. Add “from” only if there is room; he will know who it is from.

What if he is a new dad?

Mark the year: “Dad, est.” and the year the first child was born.
