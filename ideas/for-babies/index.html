Personalized baby gifts

Personalized gift ideas for a new baby: what to record, when to order and how to spell-check a newborn.

A baby gift is really for the parents, and later for the grown child. The details worth recording are the ones nobody remembers accurately in ten years: the time, the weight, the length, the place. Blankets, prints and boxes all carry them well.

Do not order until the name is announced and you have seen it written down. Names change in the first week more often than anyone admits, and middle names arrive late.

What to put on it

  • Full name, date, time, weight and length, from the parents
  • The birth flower or birthstone for the month
  • A line from you, dated, for the child to read at eighteen

Questions people ask

When should I order a personalized baby gift?

After the birth announcement. Allow about two weeks for stitched or engraved pieces. If you need something for the hospital visit, print a birth details sheet or a monogram today and frame it.

Which details go on a birth print?

Name, date and time of birth, weight, length and place. Ask whether the family uses pounds and ounces or kilograms.
