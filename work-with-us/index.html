Work with us

We commission freelance work as budget allows, funded by supporters and sponsors. Tell us what you do and we keep you in mind for the next brief.

Who we look for

  • Writers who can explain a practical thing in plain words and check their facts.
  • Illustrators for original gift drawings, motifs and printable designs.
  • Front-end developers to build small, fast, accessible browser tools.
  • Video makers who can show a craft method clearly in a few minutes.
  • Translators to bring the tools to other languages.

How it works

Work is commissioned per project with a written brief, an agreed fee and a deadline. There are no unpaid trials. If nothing suitable is open, we keep your details on file and contact you when something is.

Applicants must be 18 or over. Details are used only to consider you for work.
