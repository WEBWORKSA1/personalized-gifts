Contest rules

  1. Organiser. The contest is run by Personalized.Gifts.
  2. Eligibility. Open to individuals aged 18 or over. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. People involved in running the site may not enter.
  3. No purchase. Entry is free. No purchase, payment or donation is needed, and none improves your chance of winning.
  4. How to enter. Submit one original line through the form on this page during the round. One entry per person per round. Late, duplicate or automated entries are not counted.
  5. Entries. Your entry must be your own work, must not copy published lyrics, poems or slogans, and must not be offensive or name a private person without consent.
  6. Judging. This is a contest of skill. The editors choose one winner per round on originality, fit to the brief and usefulness on a real gift. The decision is final.
  7. Prize. The prize for each round is the one stated on this page while the round is open. Prizes are not transferable and have no cash alternative unless stated. Any taxes are the winner’s responsibility.
  8. Notification. The winner is contacted at the email address given. If there is no reply within 14 days, another entry may be chosen.
  9. Publication. By entering you allow us to publish your entry on this site with the credit you chose. You keep ownership.
  10. Personal information. Details are used only to run the contest, as described in the privacy policy.
  11. Changes. We may amend, pause or cancel a round if circumstances require, and will say so on this page.
  12. No association. The contest is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by Google, YouTube or any advertiser shown on the site. It has no connection with adverts on the site, and nothing here asks you to view or click on them.

Questions people ask

Who can enter?

Anyone aged 18 or over, anywhere it is lawful to do so. One entry per person per round.

Does it cost anything?

No. There is nothing to buy and no payment of any kind.

How is the winner chosen?

The editors judge on originality, fit to the brief and whether it would work on a real gift. Entries are judged without names attached.

Who owns my entry?

You do. Entering gives us permission to publish it on this site with the credit you choose.
